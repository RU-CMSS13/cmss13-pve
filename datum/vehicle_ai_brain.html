/datum/vehicle_ai_brain

Drives and fights with a multitile vehicle through its human AI crew, in the same way as human AI.

The brain weighs /datum/vehicle_ai_action each tick and runs the best ones that don't conflict. A seat's job is only done while a conscious AI crewman sits in it, so killing the driver stops the vehicle and killing the gunner silences its guns. A player in a seat does that seat's job instead of the AI.
