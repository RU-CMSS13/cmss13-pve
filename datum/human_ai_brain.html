(/mob/living/carbon/human/target, /datum/reagents/dose, amount, stack_doses)

Returns TRUE if giving the target amount units of the dose is safe

The dose must not push any of its reagents over its overdose threshold. Unless stack_doses is set, it also must not be given while an earlier dose of the same reagent is still working, or alongside another painkiller, as those don't stack. More units of a reagent only make it work for longer, so the next dose waits until a third of the last one is left.
